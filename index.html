About AIMD (Additive Increase, Multiplicative Decrease)

AIMD is the feedback-control algorithm used by TCP congestion avoidance. It dynamically probes for available network bandwidth by increasing transmission rate conservatively and backing off aggressively when congestion is detected.

Additive Increase (AI)
When all packets in an RTT are successfully acknowledged, cwnd increases linearly by α (+1 segment).
Multiplicative Decrease (MD)
When congestion occurs (candidate window ≥ threshold), cwnd is reduced multiplicatively by factor β (×0.5).
